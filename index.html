Industrial use cases

Designed for work where answers need context—and processes need structure.

Use RAGTerminal as a working surface for teams building retrieval-assisted knowledge workflows, internal tools and repeatable AI operations.

01 / KNOWLEDGE OPERATIONS

Enterprise and technical knowledge

Organize policies, product documentation, procedures and technical references into a clearer foundation for retrieval-based experiences.

02 / CUSTOMER OPERATIONS

Support and service teams

Structure FAQs, escalation guidance and product context so support-oriented assistants can be built from more maintainable source material.

03 / AI & DATA ENGINEERING

RAG pipeline development

Work through ingestion, retrieval, reranking and response stages as a connected workflow rather than disconnected experiments.

04 / RESEARCH

Research and analysis

Keep source collections, processing steps and repeatable research routines organized as the work evolves.

05 / PLATFORM TEAMS

Reusable internal tooling

Package recurring logic and scripts into building blocks that other workflows can draw on and improve.

06 / DELIVERY & OPERATIONS

Repeatable team procedures

Represent common sequences as plays, giving recurring tasks a shared starting point and a structure worth refining.
